Work

A short list. Depth over volume.

01

Chrono

A legal billing prototype for a contingency securities practice: time capture with approval, effective-dated rates, pre-bill through send, payments, and lodestar reporting. Built as a zero-dependency Node service with SQLite so the money path stays inspectable.

The interesting parts are the boring ones — integer cents, 15-minute rounding, immutable sent invoices, and reports that export as real Excel numbers.

02

Website 0.1

This site. Four pages, one stylesheet, no framework. Vite for local development and a static build. The contact form checks the note locally, then opens a drafted email — no backend, no inbox to babysit on day one.

It is meant to be a clean main: easy to restyle, easy to replace, and honest about what it is.
