Location & privacy

Wander Guide uses your device location to describe the area around you, like a walking tour guide.

  • GPS stays on your phone except when looking up the current area.
  • Coordinates are sent only to this app’s server, which queries public OpenStreetMap and Wikipedia data.
  • The server logs only a coarse ~1 km grid, never exact GPS, card data, or identifiers.
  • Nothing is stored as a history of where you walked.
  • You can use demo cities instead of sharing live location.
